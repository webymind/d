01 — HOW WE ARE

TERRAX HOLDINGS MAURITIUS LTD

Building Quality. Creating Value.

At TERRAX HOLDINGS MAURITIUS LTD, we believe exceptional construction starts long before ground is broken. We were founded on a simple commitment: to deliver reliable, high-quality project solutions while building genuine, long-term relationships with our clients, partners, and local communities.

We bring order, clarity, and discipline to every stage of the building process. From early site assessment and careful planning through to procurement, execution, and final handover, our structured management ensures your vision is delivered without compromise.
